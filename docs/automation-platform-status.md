## M4m eight-minute source-pinned feedback evidence gate — 2026-10-31

The application now provides a **read-only, fixed 480-second qualification** through `--vm-qualify-feedback <lab1-or-lab2> <reviewed-source-pin>` (version 0.5.5). It samples the captured pin path about every 500 ms using `Stopwatch`, requires a source-bound initial observation, observes a fresh terminal poll at or beyond 480,000 ms and returns one aggregate, redacted `VmSustainedFeedbackReport`. Its versioned policy is `rr2-pinned-480s-gap-strictly-below-4500-v1`: each measured interval between accepted producer advances must be strictly **less than 4,500 ms**, including a gap closed by the next advance and the terminal tail. Accepted progress requires rising outer, addon sequence and clock; duplicate receipt delivery and stationary XYZ do not masquerade as movement. A source error, identity change, sequence contradiction, excessive gap, early cancellation or incomplete window fails the attempt permanently. This is telemetry evidence, not action readiness, control authorization or gameplay causality.

The gate deliberately allows *narrow* recoverable freshness-only polls when source/producer identity still verifies, so it does not silently reject the kind of bounded not-fresh polls present in RR2's previously accepted Lab 2 window. Reader parsing now attaches nonserialized provenance and producer metadata to selected expired/stale receipts **only after** validating bound/selected state, completed scan, finite nonnegative source age, positive freshness timeout, valid producer sequence/epoch/clock/XYZ, source-pin identity and game target. The qualifier compares the whole IP/PID/start/path/hash tuple, stream/player fingerprint and addon epoch to its original baseline, and retains a separate transport/producer watermark even while XYZ remains unavailable. A stale receipt with invalid inner timing, unbound report, different source or regressed transport cannot be laundered into a harmless gap. An unknown/unverifiable interruption is a failure rather than an invented continuation.
